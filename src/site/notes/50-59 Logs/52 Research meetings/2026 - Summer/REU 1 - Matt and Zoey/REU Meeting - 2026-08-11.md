This following is a brief summary of our research meeting on 2026-08-11.

## Meeting summary
---

We briefly went over answers to the following questions:
- What are Lie’s three theorems? (The First Theorem is tough to understand!)
- Can a given group G always be equipped with a Lie group structure? Can it be equipped with different Lie group structures? (It's not clear for arbitrary groups, but definitely true for finite groups. And different groups can indeed be given different Lie group structures.)
- Which topological spaces can be equipped with a Lie group structure? (This answer is not super obvious in general, but the best answer seems to be if it's a topological manifold.)
- Can non-isomorphic Lie groups have isomorphic Lie algebras? (Yep!)
- What is Lie integration? (We talked briefly about this.)
- Is there a classification of all Lie groups? (Sort of. If you add adjectives like "compact" or "simple", then yes.)

We then spent the rest of the time talking about the 7-minute presentation.
