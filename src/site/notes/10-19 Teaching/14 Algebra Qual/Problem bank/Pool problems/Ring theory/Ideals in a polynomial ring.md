Let $R$ be a commutative ring with $1$. Use theorems in ring theory to prove:
(a) $\langle x\rangle$ is a prime ideal in $R[x]$ if and only if $R$ is an integral domain.
(b) $\langle x\rangle$ is a maximal ideal in $R[x]$ if and only if $R$ is a field.
