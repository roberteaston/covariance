Consider the additive group of integers ${\bf Z}$.
(a) Prove that every subgroup of ${\bf Z}$ is a cyclic group.
(b) Prove that every homomorphic image of ${\bf Z}$ is a cyclic group.
(c) Now consider the *ring* ${\bf Z}$. Exhibit a prime ideal of ${\bf Z}$ that is not maximal.
