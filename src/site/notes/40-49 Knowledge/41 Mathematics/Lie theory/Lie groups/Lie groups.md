## General Lie groups
---

> [!note] Definition of a Lie group
> A **Lie group** is a smooth manifold whose underlying set of elements is equipped with the structure of a group such that the group multiplication and inverse-assigning functions are smooth maps.

In other words, a Lie group is a group object internal to the category of smooth manifolds.
